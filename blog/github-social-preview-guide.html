How to Set Your Social Preview

  1. Go to your repository on GitHub
  2. Click Settings
  3. Scroll down to Social preview
  4. Click Edit and upload your 1280×640 PNG image
  5. Click Save

That's it. Next time someone shares your repo link, the custom image appears automatically.
